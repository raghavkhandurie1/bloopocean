Secret collection

Every secret you find lands here.

  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Bloop Ocean doesn't collect your name, email or anything else about you.
  • Your progress stays on your device. Spins, prizes, secrets and sound settings are saved in your browser's local storage. They are never sent anywhere.
  • All sounds are made live in your browser. Nothing is recorded, and the page never asks for your microphone or camera.
  • Fonts come from Google Fonts, and the page is hosted on claude.ai. Both may see standard connection details like your IP address under their own privacy policies.
  • To erase everything, use Start over, or clear this site's data in your browser settings.
